Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Vertex Games ("Vertex Games", "we", "us", or "our") may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit our websites.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them.

Cookies can allow a website to remember information about your visit, maintain certain functionality, improve security, and provide a better user experience.

Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, and other technologies that perform functions similar to cookies.

2. HOW VERTEX GAMES USES COOKIES

Vertex Games may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Essential website functionality
  • Security
  • Remembering preferences
  • Maintaining website functionality
  • Understanding website performance
  • Analytics, where implemented
  • Other features necessary to operate our website

The technologies actually used on a particular Vertex Games website may vary depending on the website and features provided.

3. ESSENTIAL COOKIES

Essential cookies or similar technologies may be necessary for certain website functions.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Maintain basic website functionality
  • Remember necessary settings
  • Maintain security
  • Detect abuse
  • Support navigation
  • Enable features requested by the user

Because these technologies may be necessary for the operation of the website, they may not always be disabled through the website itself.

4. PREFERENCE COOKIES

Where implemented, preference cookies may allow our website to remember choices you make.

Examples may include:

  • Language preferences
  • Display preferences
  • Other website settings

These cookies are intended to make future visits more convenient.

5. ANALYTICS COOKIES

If we use analytics services, analytics technologies may help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

This may include information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Approximate visit duration
  • General navigation patterns
  • Device and browser information
  • General website performance information

Analytics technologies will only be used where they are actually implemented on the applicable website.

If analytics are not implemented, no analytics cookies are placed merely because they are described in this policy.

6. ADVERTISING AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

Vertex Games does not automatically use advertising or marketing cookies simply because they are described in this Cookie Policy.

If advertising, marketing, or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, we may update this Cookie Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

Third-party advertising providers may use their own technologies when such services are implemented.

7. THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

Some third-party services embedded in or linked from our website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Examples may include:

  • Analytics providers
  • Video providers
  • Social media services
  • Embedded content providers
  • Security services
  • Other third-party services

The use of cookies by third parties is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Vertex Games does not control cookies placed directly by third-party services.

8. CURRENT WEBSITE TECHNOLOGIES

Our website is designed primarily as a static website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

At the time this Cookie Policy was last updated, we aim to limit the use of unnecessary tracking technologies.

If additional technologies are introduced, this policy may be updated accordingly.

9. MANAGING COOKIES

Most modern web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings.

You may be able to:

  • Block cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Allow cookies only from certain websites
  • Receive notifications before cookies are stored
  • Block third-party cookies

The exact controls available depend on your browser and device.

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.

10. MOBILE DEVICES

Our games and mobile applications may use technologies that are different from traditional website cookies.

For example, applications may use:

  • Local storage
  • Device identifiers
  • Authentication tokens
  • Application preferences
  • Other technical identifiers

These technologies may be covered by our Privacy Policy and are not necessarily browser cookies.

11. COOKIES AND OUR GAMES

Cookies primarily relate to our websites.

Our games and applications may use other technologies for functionality such as:

  • Authentication
  • Saving preferences
  • Online connectivity
  • Multiplayer functionality
  • Security
  • Analytics, where implemented
  • Crash reporting, where implemented

Information relating to game and application data practices is described in our Privacy Policy.

12. EPIC ONLINE SERVICES

Certain Vertex Games products may use Epic Online Services ("EOS") for online functionality.

EOS functionality may involve authentication, multiplayer services, matchmaking, social features, or other online services depending on the game.

The technologies used by Epic Online Services are not necessarily browser cookies.

Information processed through Epic Online Services is addressed in our Privacy Policy and may also be subject to Epic Games' own policies and terms.

13. CHANGES TO THIS COOKIE POLICY

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time.

Changes may occur due to:

  • Changes to our website

  • New third-party services
  • Changes to our data practices

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
